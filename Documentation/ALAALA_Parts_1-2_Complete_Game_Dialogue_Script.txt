EXTRACTION AND READING NOTES

Source: current Unity working tree. Filipino and English dialogue are reproduced without correction, translation, or speaker renaming.
Scope: all serialized character dialogue in current gameplay sources, relevant runtime-created dialogue, and story narration. Non-dialogue menus, tutorials, inventory descriptions, Journal cards, task objectives, debug messages, and decorative sleep glyphs are excluded.
Static source inspection does not prove every conversation is reachable. Each block records its wiring or uncertainty. No Play Mode was entered.
SCENE numbering is a document index, not an invented Unity identifier. Root-node connections establish the order inside a conversation; choice branches remain separate. Independent free-roam conversations have no single required order.
Text between quotation delimiters is copied from the source. Meaningful embedded line breaks, rich-text markup, trailing spaces inside dialogue, spelling, punctuation, capitalization, and speaker identifiers are preserved. YAML wrapping is decoded rather than copied as a spoken line break.
A source component fileID is reported when no standalone conversation identifier exists. A shared asset is transcribed once and its known usages are listed. Scene-local copies remain separate contexts.
The scripts Part1ReturnTransportController and Part1PosadaEndingController use Part1 names for the return after Anday. Their names are retained in traceability; the return is placed after the Anday events in this document.
Padre Agaton is present as an actor/reference in the implemented Anday recollection. That does not establish a later playable story part or authorize dialogue from older drafts.
